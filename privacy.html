Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who we are

GGGGazette ("we", "the service") is a small, privately operated project that automatically generates and publishes short news videos. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. What this service does

The service is operated by its owner. It is not a consumer app: visitors do not create accounts and we do not run advertising or tracking on this website. The software connects to the owner's own accounts on social platforms (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, LinkedIn, Bluesky, X, Twitch) through their official APIs in order to upload videos and to read statistics (such as views and likes) of the videos we published ourselves.

3. Data we process

  • Authorisation tokens (OAuth access and refresh tokens) for the owner's own accounts, stored privately on the owner's server and never published or shared.
  • Statistics of our own content, such as view and like counts, retrieved through the platform APIs.
  • Public news sources used as input for scripts. These are public articles and headlines, not personal data of our users.
  • Technical logs (timestamps, errors, API usage) kept on the owner's server for troubleshooting.

We do not collect personal data from visitors of this website and we do not use cookies or analytics on it. If you comment on or interact with our videos on a platform, that interaction is handled by that platform under its own privacy policy.

4. Google / YouTube API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the YouTube API only to upload our own videos and to read analytics of our own channel. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties, except as needed to operate the service. By using YouTube features you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke our access to your Google data at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

5. Third-party processors

To produce videos, text, images and voice-overs, content such as news text and generated scripts is sent to AI and media services, including Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), ElevenLabs and Hugging Face. These providers process it under their own terms. We do not send personal data of visitors to them.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Logs and generated files are kept only as long as needed for operation and are deleted periodically.

7. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to processing. Because we do not intentionally collect personal data about visitors, this will rarely apply, but you can contact us at any time at CONTACT_EMAIL and we will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
